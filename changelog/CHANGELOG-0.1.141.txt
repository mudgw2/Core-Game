CORE 0.1.141

Fixed planetary test-world presentation and controls:
- Spawn location now corresponds to the center of the global map (0° latitude, 0° longitude).
- Camera position is clamped above sampled terrain during gameplay.
- Increased camera far clipping and made background stars independent of scene fog.
- Added a deterministic presentation-only natural satellite to provide a visible night-sky body until generated moon records exist.
- Replaced the planetary map modal with a compact floating HUD.
- Added regression assertions for spawn, camera clearance, map presentation, sky visibility, and M/C/I keyboard bindings.

No universe generation, fingerprints, saved universe records, character records, or player data were changed.
