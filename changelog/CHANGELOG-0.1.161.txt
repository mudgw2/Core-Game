CORE 0.1.161
First-person camera and moon visibility correction

- Added distinct third-person, close, and first-person camera modes.
- Mouse-wheel zoom can enter first-person; zooming outward returns to close view.
- F cycles third-person → close → first-person → third-person.
- First-person camera follows eye height, hides the local character model, and bypasses third-person terrain clearance while active.
- Reframed moons to a low local-horizon arc so they can appear in the default camera frustum.
- Increased moon visual scale and emissive readability, marked moon meshes as always active/visible, and extended camera far clipping and widened the default field of view.
- Preserved data-driven moon period/phase and the 0.1.160 jump animation behavior.
