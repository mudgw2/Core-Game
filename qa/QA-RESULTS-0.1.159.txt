Implementation checks:
- Fixed a direct runtime exception: the moon update referenced the removed identifier `moonAngle` after the orbital calculation was split into `orbitalAngle` and `phaseAngle`. Worlds with moons could therefore abort the astronomical update loop. Moon mesh rotation now uses the defined `phaseAngle`.
- Moon placement now uses a stable local sky frame initialized from the entry camera heading and local surface normal. The former global X/Z orbital plane could put the moon behind terrain or outside the default camera frustum.
- Moon visual diameter cap increased to 280 scene units and a restrained emissive component improves night-side readability.
- The planetary camera field of view is widened to 1.15 radians to include the near-horizon moon arc.
